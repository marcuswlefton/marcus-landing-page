Case study · Commercial performance

His strongest Q1
in five years.

David V. · Earlier engagement · Three-month account

~200%of plan, reported by the client
“Within three months, I had my best Q1 ever, hitting almost 200% of plan — the highest I’ve performed in five years.”

The problem to solve

Make strong commercial performance more repeatable while improving the relationship between demanding work, recovery, and life at home.

What I looked at

When his best attention was available, what work occupied those hours, and how the working day ended. We examined the fit between the work and the state he brought to it.

The judgment

Increasing effort was one possible response. A more useful hypothesis to test was whether his best capacity was reaching his highest-value work.

What changed

We matched work to state, protected his best hours for higher-value work, moved lower-value demands, planned recovery, and established a shutdown ritual.

What he reported

Nearly 200% of plan after three months and his strongest Q1 in five years. The work also emphasized staying engaged at home.

What this establishes

A client-reported outcome during an earlier engagement. There is no controlled comparison or independent audit, so this does not isolate the effect of advisory or predict a 30-day result.

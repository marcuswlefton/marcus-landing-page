## Measurement now in place

- Campaign source plus first selected essay viewed in the current tab reaches the existing Kit source fields on form submission.
- Applications also carry an allowlisted same-tab page journey and the optional answer to “What prompted you to reach out?”
- Kit fields created: `advisory_prompted_by`, `advisory_journey`, `advisory_stage`.
- Use `advisory_stage` manually: Enquiry, Qualified call, Paid, or Not a fit. The website deliberately does not overwrite an existing stage when someone reapplies.
- A qualified call means a plausible fit for the $5,000 engagement, a consequential problem Marcus can address, and a reason to act. A title or company alone is insufficient.
- Weekly, compare qualified calls and paid engagements by source and essay. Review what applicants say prompted contact alongside the technical path.

## Limits and next measurement gate

This implementation records the path for people who submit, not the paths of all visitors. It does not establish a visitor-to-application conversion rate. Search Console supplies search impressions/clicks after verification and processing. A complete cross-channel visitor funnel needs an existing or separately configured analytics property. No new paid analytics service, GA account, tracking consent system, or broad anonymous event collection was added.

Session storage does not identify people across devices, browsers, or closed tabs. Browser privacy restrictions may remove attribution; they do not prevent applying. Real Kit delivery must be verified separately from simulated transport tests. Data should be read as directional attribution, not proof that one article caused a purchase.

A few practical questions.

What makes this different from a performance program?

We begin with your intended result and examine the person, skills, and surrounding systems together. The work follows the constraint the evidence points toward. That may involve physical capacity, decision-making, skill development, work design, or a combination. Your Performance Map changes as we learn what works in your circumstances.

What is the difference between the fit call and the Intensive?

The complimentary 15-minute conversation helps us decide whether to work together, with no obligation to proceed. The paid Intensive includes the deeper review, intervention design, testing, and Performance Map.

How often do we meet, and what happens between sessions?

The cadence is tailored to the decisions and tests your engagement requires. Before payment, we agree the session schedule, the time to set aside for implementation, and the channel and response window for written feedback. Between sessions, you test changes in your actual week and record what happens in your private workspace. I review the feedback and help refine the next move.

Does every engagement cover all six dimensions?

We examine the connections that matter to your result. The work may concentrate on a small number of changes once the evidence points to a useful place to intervene.

What outcomes can you promise?

A defined process and tangible working outputs. Business and personal outcomes depend on your context, implementation, and factors beyond the engagement. Earlier client results describe individual experiences and include partnerships of different lengths.

What happens after the 30 days?

You leave with the working map, evidence, and next sequence. Any further advisory work is a separate conversation, with its scope and fee agreed in writing.
